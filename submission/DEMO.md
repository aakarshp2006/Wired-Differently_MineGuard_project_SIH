Once recorded, the demo video will cover:

1. The physical MineGuard prototype (vehicle, sensors, ESP32-CAM)
2. Manual vehicle movement under driver control
3. Live distance sensing (HC-SR04) and speed telemetry (LM393 encoder) on the dashboard
4. YOLO-based object detection on the live camera feed
5. Fog / low-visibility detection output
6. The three risk states — SAFE, WARNING, and DANGER — shown on the dashboard
7. The automatic emergency stop triggering under a critical-risk condition
